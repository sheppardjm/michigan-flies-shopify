# Home surface brief

Scope: the home route (`src/app/page.tsx`) plus the shared shell (header, footer, global tokens and fonts) that every page inherits. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a traveling angler planning a Michigan trip, at a desk weeks out or on a phone the week of. They must grasp in seconds that the site names the fly for a river on a date and can prove it. Primary action: start the fly finder. Then: what is hatching now. Then: the most popular flies.

Proof carried with real material only: live USGS water temperatures and gridMET degree days, dated and attributed to gauges; hatch timing with visible S/A/I evidence tags; credited iNaturalist insect photographs. No testimonials, counts, or claims the data does not hold. Popular flies come from the Two Hearted batch until sales data exists and say so.

Untouched: fly finder logic and data; inner pages' layouts (they inherit tokens); store pages; factual copy. Anti-goals: photo-hero outdoor storefront; badges as decoration on a white page; a chalkboard that reads as a menu; invented claims; a kicker above any heading.

Open decisions: the final wordmark (the script face is a placeholder for commissioned lettering) and the engraved SVG plates (placeholders sized for commissioned art; the leaping trout logo on the sign and footer is the owner's own file, supplied September 28, 2026, and is not a placeholder); the reserve email address; all 44 river badges are shown, grouped by region, pending the owner's call.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the back wall of a Michigan fly shop working as an instrument: the woodcut trout sign over the counter, the order card on the counter, and the hatch board chalked with today's live numbers. It refuses the category arrangement of river photo hero, shop-by-category tiles, and a finder demoted to a menu item.

OWN-WORLD (owner revision, September 29, 2026: the owner's own Two Hearted photographs now carry the grounds: the river valley faded behind the first viewport, a dark band of hero catches over the run below camp, the river's edge faint under the shelf, and the tannin water under the print row; the engraved river bend is retired from the home page): Rainbow trout palette on shop materials, rendered in a field-guide register (owner revision, September 28, 2026: the woodcut header, the screen-printed wallpaper, and the stitched badges were judged childish and replaced). Cream-belly painted shiplap as the wall (planks faint, grain light), olive-back as the primary and the dark green chalkboard, rose lateral band as the single accent, copper fin for focus and hover, ink black for type, dirt brown for the rails and the engraving ink. A refined slab (Zilla Slab) for the sign line and section titles, a sign-painter script (Mr Dafoe) as the wordmark placeholder until lettering is commissioned, a geometric sans for badges, labels, and body, a typewriter mono reserved for live numbers and dates only. The mark is the owner's leaping rainbow trout logo (`public/photos/illustration/trout-logo.svg`, full-color, transparent), carried on the enamel sign and the footer rail. Supporting illustration is engraved line art in one ink, authored as SVG and sized as slots for commissioned art: a northern Michigan river bend through white pine and cedar on the rear plane behind the first viewport, a Michigan plate with all 44 rivers plotted at their real coordinates beside the shelf, a white pine stand over the footer. River badges follow the Campbell Outfitters manner: solid fill, ink edge, two solid inset keylines, a small mark per region, caps name and a script sub-line; one silhouette per region. Chalk marks in four named states (approaching dashed, hatching underlined, peak filled rose, finished struck), roughened by an SVG chalk filter. Four depth planes: engraved plate, shiplap, board and badges, counter card; the plate drifts on scroll, the front plane holds still. No repeating pattern anywhere; browser surfaces themed from the palette.

STORY: A planner arrives, reads the sign, sees today's date and real water temperatures chalked on the board next to the hatches, and understands this is live and local. They fill the counter card and press Find my flies. Scrolling, they meet the shelf of river badges (their river is there), the popular flies with the real insect beside each, and the evidence legend that tells them how much to trust every line.
