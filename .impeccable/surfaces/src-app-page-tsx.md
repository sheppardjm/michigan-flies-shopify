# Home surface brief

Scope: the home route (`src/app/page.tsx`) plus the shared shell (header, footer, global tokens and fonts) that every page inherits. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a traveling angler planning a Michigan trip, at a desk weeks out or on a phone the week of. They must grasp in seconds that the site names the fly for a river on a date and can prove it. Primary action: start the fly finder. Then: what is hatching now. Then: the most popular flies.

Proof carried with real material only: live USGS water temperatures and gridMET degree days, dated and attributed to gauges; hatch timing with visible S/A/I evidence tags; credited iNaturalist insect photographs. No testimonials, counts, or claims the data does not hold. Popular flies come from the Two Hearted batch until sales data exists and say so.

Untouched: fly finder logic and data; inner pages' layouts (they inherit tokens); store pages; factual copy. Anti-goals: photo-hero outdoor storefront; badges as decoration on a white page; a chalkboard that reads as a menu; invented claims; a kicker above any heading.

Open decisions: the final wordmark (script lettering is a placeholder until a mark is commissioned); the reserve email address; all 44 river badges are shown, grouped by region, pending the owner's call.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the back wall of a Michigan fly shop working as an instrument: the woodcut trout sign over the counter, the order card on the counter, and the hatch board chalked with today's live numbers. It refuses the category arrangement of river photo hero, shop-by-category tiles, and a finder demoted to a menu item.

OWN-WORLD: Rainbow trout palette on shop materials. Cream-belly painted shiplap as the wall (light, for phones in daylight), olive-back as the primary and the dark green chalkboard, rose lateral band as the single accent, copper fin for focus and hover, ink black for type and spots, dirt brown for the rails. Woodtype slab for the sign and section titles, a brush sign-painter script for the wordmark placeholder, a geometric sans for badges, labels, and body, a typewriter mono reserved for live numbers and dates only. Materials: SVG woodcut trout, screen-printed wallpaper of flies, hooks, fish, pines, and rocks in earthen hues, grain noise on the wall, embroidered-edge river badges, chalk marks in four named states (approaching dashed, hatching underlined, peak filled rose, finished struck). Four depth planes: wallpaper, shiplap, board and badges, counter card; rear planes drift on scroll, the front plane holds still. No unpainted area on any viewport; browser surfaces (selection, focus, scrollbar, caret) themed from the palette.

STORY: A planner arrives, reads the sign, sees today's date and real water temperatures chalked on the board next to the hatches, and understands this is live and local. They fill the counter card and press Find my flies. Scrolling, they meet the shelf of river badges (their river is there), the popular flies with the real insect beside each, and the evidence legend that tells them how much to trust every line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed wall. Top rail: script wordmark left, nav right, all on a dirt-brown wood rail. Center top: the woodcut trout enamel sign with "Michigan Flies" in script and "Hand-tied for Michigan rivers" in woodtype beneath. Left two-thirds below the sign: the counter card, four fields on a cream card with a pencil-ruled look (River, Date, Fish, Setup) and one large olive button, Find my flies. Right third: the hatch board, dark green, "Hatching now" in chalk woodtype, today's date in mono, then per-region rows of hatches in the state vocabulary, and a chalked column of live water temperatures for gauged rivers with the gauge name and reading time. On phones the same order stacks: sign, counter card, board; the wall still runs edge to edge.

FORM: The Shop Wall, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned by the roll; the top-ranked Hatch Board was offered as the pick and not taken). Seed key b262ba5f. Raises kept from the beaten hand, each binding: real depth planes (multiplane), no unpainted area (lowbrow panel), one reserved live color (teletext), strict state vocabulary (glaze shelf), one badge voice per region under a shared timeline (night market). Code-led: no comp; the signature interaction is the four-plane scroll parallax with a still front card, and the motion grammar is chalk marks drawing on once as the board enters view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
